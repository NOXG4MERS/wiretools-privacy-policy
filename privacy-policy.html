Privacy Policy for WireToolS

Effective date: October 16, 2026
App: WireToolS (package: com.wiretools.app)
Contact: satoriwire@gmail.com

Overview

WireToolS is an all-in-one tools app. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. In short: WireToolS does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or password, and most tools process your data entirely on your device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell personal information on our servers. WireToolS has no developer-operated backend that receives your data. Any content you create or edit with the app (documents, images, videos, files) stays on your device unless you choose to share or upload it yourself.

Permissions and How They Are Used

WireToolS requests permissions only when a tool actually needs them (just-in-time), and only for the purpose of that tool:

  • Location — used only while you are actively using a location-based tool (for example, GPS track recording). Location is processed on your device and is not sent to us. Location is never collected in the background.
  • Camera — used only when you use a tool that captures photos (for example, scanning or image tools).
  • Photos, media, and files — used only when you open, edit, convert, or save files with a tool you chose.
  • Internet access — used only by tools that need online data to function (for example, IP lookup or online checkers). When such a tool runs, the minimum data needed for that tool to work may be sent to the relevant third-party service (for example, an IP address for a geolocation lookup). That data is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

Some tools rely on third-party services to perform their function. Those services may process limited technical data as required by the tool. We do not combine that data with any personal information, because we do not collect personal information.

Data Retention

Because your data stays on your device, we do not retain it on any server. If you delete the app or the files it created, that data is gone.

Children's Privacy

WireToolS is a general-audience utility app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at the email address above and we will address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this same location with a revised effective date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests related to your data, contact us at satoriwire@gmail.com.
